# Product Benchmark

## Useful Planning Platform Direction

The app should become a practical delivery planning platform, not just a reporting dashboard.

The strongest product direction is:

- Contract setup and governance
- Current, previous, and baseline programme control
- Live look-ahead planning
- Weekly work planning
- Daily coordination and meeting mode
- Resource and capacity planning
- Area / WBS / spatial coordination views
- Board, Gantt, area, and list views
- Owner assignment directly from planning cards and list rows
- Additional look-ahead activities created inside the planning workspace
- Constraints, blockers, decisions, and handoffs
- Branded reports and NEC narratives
- Audit trail and saved project records

## Add

- Daily meeting mode for decisions, blockers, handoffs, and owner actions
- Multiple planning views: board, Gantt, area map, list
- Task owner assignment from the plan view
- Manually added planning activities that are clearly marked as added, not imported
- Constraints register linked to planned activities
- Daily meeting actions with owner, due date, and linked activity
- Mobile-style activity update flow for quick site/planner input
- Audit trail for assignments, updates, constraints, actions, and uploads
- Resource planning by owner, discipline, labour, plant, and materials
- Named people and resource register per contract
- Capacity and availability visible against each person
- Mobile-friendly daily update flow
- Report pack builder with selected outputs and saved history
- AI readiness check before generating narratives
- Contract-level setup wizard for reusable configuration

## Remove or Hide

- Placeholder buttons that do not perform a real action
- Hardcoded contract numbers or HPC-specific terminology in the generic product
- Duplicate upload routes
- Admin tools from normal user workflows
- AI buttons before the required source data is available

## Principle

Every view should help the delivery team answer one of four questions:

- What is the plan?
- What changed?
- What is blocking us?
- Who needs to act today?
